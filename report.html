6. Limits

  • Settlement price is not a fill price; volume is not liquidity. Thin far-month contracts can show spreads you could never trade.
  • Sample is 35 trading days, so results are descriptive, not statistically strong. GOLDTEN history only starts in 2025.
  • The GOLDM purity adjustment (999/995) is an assumption to validate against MCX contract specs.
  • GOLDPETAL is quoted per gram in whole rupees, so its price per 10 g moves in steps of Rs 10; small spreads are partly rounding.
  • Costs are an assumed flat 5 bps; real brokerage, exchange fees, taxes and margin funding differ.
  • Analysis only. Not investment advice.
